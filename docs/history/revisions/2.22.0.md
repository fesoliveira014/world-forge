# Atlas 2.22.0 — categorical regions with terrain relief

River basins, River sources, Biomes, and Marshes & peatlands now remove redundant
interior triangles while continuing to follow the saved terrain. They retain
the user's relief setting on the globe and in Shaded relief maps. Tectonic
plates remain the only height-independent categorical view.

## Geometry and data

The four views use the same deterministic region-collapse engine as tectonic
plates, with additional height constraints. Each collapse keeps a cumulative
envelope of the original displayed scalar elevations affected by that operation,
including faces removed by earlier collapses. The entire envelope must span no
more than 5 metres. Steep terrain and narrow ridges therefore retain their
original geometry. Retained directions and raw height values are copied exactly
from the original packet; the ocean display still floors negative heights to
sea level.

New edges have a fixed maximum angular length of 0.5 degrees. This limit controls
spherical curvature separately from the scalar elevation envelope: an edge of
that length has approximately 61 metres of midpoint chord sag on an Earth-sized
sphere. The 5-metre constraint is not a bound on the total Cartesian difference
between faceted spheres. Existing coarse edges remain unchanged. Geometry is
prepared once for the layer and season, independently of the camera and zoom.

Only interiors of the same semantic category and exact packed color can merge.
River basins use full system IDs rather than their repeating palette index.
Biome and wetland types retain their own boundaries, and lake IDs keep separate
water bodies distinct. Packet boundaries stay exact. Partial-water cell fans,
delta cell fans, and partially flooded corner vertices are pinned; original lake
surface heights remain in place. The source world and simulation fields are
never modified.

The manifold link condition and outward-winding check preserve topology during
each collapse. Packets with less than 2% triangle reduction, or with no reduction
in payload bytes, keep their original buffers. Mountainous or category-rich
areas consequently benefit less than ocean and nearly level region interiors.
Existing spatial batching, hidden-face culling, and GPU multi-draw submissions
remain in use. No camera-dependent detail replacement or new disk cache is added.

Geographic picking derives globe coordinates from the visible terrain hit and
undoes the flat map's height shear before inspecting the original saved cell.
This avoids interpolating directions through larger display triangles. Renderer
diagnostics distinguish prepared source triangles from projection-added seam
faces. The tectonic simplifier retains its previous settings and output.

## Verification

- All 405 tests pass, alongside TypeScript, formatting, and changed-file lint.
- Geometry tests cover exact saved heights, cumulative elevation bounds on
  ramps, saddles and sharp ridges, manifold edges, fixed angular limits,
  deterministic output, protected faces, and unchanged-buffer fallbacks.
- Reader integration tests use a genuinely refined 3,445-cell world for all
  four views and all five season selections. They verify real simplification
  over both plateaus and ocean, exact mountain and partial-lake faces, seasonal
  water colors, preserved basin IDs that share a palette color, lake boundaries,
  per-color spherical coverage, two opposite face traversals at every output
  edge, and immutable world and saved sidecar data.
- Existing coarse-world tests retain exact original cell fans and colors. Four
  independent tectonic fixtures produce byte-identical packets after sharing
  the collapse engine.
- Picking tests cover raised map facets near a pole, map height shear, globe
  hit directions, and clipped or invalid map hits. Adaptive primal and category
  meshes still produce usable conservative horizon-culling bounds.

All 23 small/refined browser captures match the previous images pixel for pixel
across the four layers, globe and flat map, high and low relief, oblique views,
and grid/river/coast overlays. Changing relief remains visibly effective in all
four views. These comparisons use the existing 15,049-cell locally refined
fixture. Its drainage layer reduces 90,282 triangles to 87,722; other categories
can remain unchanged where the bounds prevent a useful reduction.

Inspection returns the same physical lake cell and saved height. The globe
anchor differs by about 1.50e-8 radians, while reversing the map's height shear
corrects an approximately 1.91e-5-radian difference from the previous
barycentric anchor. No shader or WebGL errors occurred in these comparisons.

same bounded geometry path, but their savings depend on terrain and category
boundaries.

pixels of a previous image edge, and 64 in the close view, all within one pixel.

approximation changes a small number of rasterized edge pixels. The tested
globe interiors contain no new background-colored holes. The retained maximum
height range remains -7,358.82 to 15,888.76 metres.

The maximum flat map submits 5,701,752 triangles, including seam faces, and
reduces native terrain draw calls from 130 to 84. Native GPU buffer storage drops
from 359,048,784 to 151,049,200 bytes; accounted display buffers drop from

all at existing image edges. Switching projection reuses resident terrain and
river packets without new physical or river requests. No runtime or WebGL
