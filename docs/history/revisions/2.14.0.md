# World Forge 2.14.0 — Adaptive physical surface detail

Adaptive refinement adds physical surface cells around coasts, river corridors,
lake outlets, complex terrain and volcanic regions before erosion. Climate and
tectonics retain the process grid. Geometric transfers conserve rainfall and
evaporation across unequal cells. Resolved spacing plans make mesh decisions
replayable. Earlier recipes default to refinement off.

The initial release used generator 2.14.0. The later
[adaptive landscape notes](../design/adaptive-surface.md) describe its extension
to evolving terrain, and [AETHER-ADAPTIVE.json](../../../examples/AETHER-ADAPTIVE.json)
retains a small example recipe.

This summary records the imported prototype milestone; current behavior is
documented in the [architecture](../../architecture.md).
