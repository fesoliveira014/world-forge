# v2.4.1: visible shallow lakes

## Cause

The retained full-resolution AETHER-43246 world from v2.4.0 contains the reported closed lake. Its water level is +2.474 m, its modeled area is about 531,392 km², and its stored water is about 57.25 km³. Its 729 wet surface cells have depths from 0.0006 to 0.215 m after continental-basin shaping.

The natural surface and ecology independently required `lakeDepth > 2`, while the lake overlay and drainage already used the actual standing water. Thus every cell in this lake was drawn as land in the natural view and classified as a land biome. Major tributaries of roughly 5,158 and 2,632 m³/s terminated at wet lake corners whose surrounding cells were all painted as desert.

## Change

Natural-world shading, lake biome classification, and exclusion of underwater riverbanks now use `lakeDepth > 0`, consistent with the lake overlay. Dry ground remains dry; a shallow lake remains standing water. Terrain construction, rainfall, lake balance, storage, levels, and receiver paths are unchanged.

The regression test exercises a shallow lake next to a desert bank and ocean, checking both ecology and the attributes supplied to natural-world shading. The supplied full world can be checked using its retained hydrology and refreshing ecology and render attributes; climate does not need to be run again for this change.

Existing v2 project JSON files, including v2.4.0, remain loadable. Reload the Site and import the project to regenerate with this correction.
