# v2.9.2 — Restored lake surfaces, blended banks, and seasonal delta cells

The preceding rendering revision was a regression. It removed the natural surface's inland-water mask and left the triangular patches the user wanted removed. Joined green ribbon geometry still produced unattractive banks; delta islands and long fan lines were also rejected. This revision replaces those display paths. Generator identity remains 2.9.0.

## Changes

- **Lakes:** restore water to the planet surface using positive lake depth or at least half-cell coverage, matching ecological lake classification. Remove `LakeSurfaces.ts` completely. Smooth layers interpolate the cell mask; categorical layers color complete cells. Lake mouths use incident visible lake cells as display anchors.
- **Banks:** remove `FloodplainStrips.ts`. Bake round-ended, softly fading strokes from the existing courses and physical bank records into one texture. Maximum coverage joins overlapping strokes without additive darkening. Premultiplied color and linear filtering avoid dark edge halos. Land masks stop vegetation at water. Both layouts use the same spherical sampling; seasons change pigment and flood tones.
- **Deltas:** remove `DeltaTiles.ts` and all rendering of branch polylines. Keep the ordinary trunk mouth. Shade up to two adjacent land cells with green-blue or brown-blue colors that vary with vegetation, rain and temperature. Natural view blends those colors; categorical views retain cell boundaries.
- **Resources:** dispose textures when the world/view is replaced. Orbiting and animation reuse the texture. Map clipping composes with the bank material hook rather than overwriting it.

Terrain, receivers, lake storage, annual and seasonal discharge, source labels, delta metadata and saved world configuration are unchanged.

## Focused observations

The retained AETHER-0004 snapshot was reused without regenerating climate. Screenshot cell 303,085 has 2.209 m of lake depth; cell 501,379 has zero sampled depth but five-sixths water coverage. Both now satisfy the visible surface water mask. The world has 19,976 lake cells and 391 delta records, represented by 688 unique delta cells.

At 655,362 surface cells, the 4,096 × 2,048 bank texture baked in 0.754 seconds and uses 32 MiB before GPU mipmaps. This replaces the old bank mesh of about two million vertices. These are one-run CPU observations, not browser frame-rate measurements.

All 22 focused checks passed, covering existing physical invariants and the revised water mask, channel joins, soft/seasonal bank texture and bounded delta cell counts. A material-hook check confirms that map clipping retains bank shading. TypeScript and the production build also pass. Browser visual testing was not performed; the supplied screenshots guided these changes.

## Limits

Lake outlines remain cell-scale; fractional wet corners below the dominant-cell threshold do not receive separate patches. Very narrow banks are softened to texture resolution. Delta cells are representative color, not extra physical islands or land area. Future settlement logic should use the model's water, bank-access and sediment fields rather than the display tint or exaggerated river width.
