# v2.1 — inland moisture, flowing water, and the restored workspace

## What changed

- Explicit conservative atmospheric mixing in m²/s replaces dependence on mesh-dependent numerical mixing. Combined advection/diffusion CFL keeps transfers stable and conservative.
- Longer moisture residence and spinup, stronger default winds, configurable evaporation/rainout, current-driven coastal SST influence, and one water-limited ET recycling pass improve inland rainfall.
- Three initialization years precede the recorded soil-water year. Storm quickflow and slow soil drainage feed tributaries instead of requiring rainfall to exceed all evaporation demand plus an empty 140 mm bucket.
- Partially wet lake boundary samples no longer fall between dry-channel and wet-footprint tests.
- Actual wind/current tracers animate over the globe. River ribbons pulse downstream with shared phase at confluences. Density, animation speed, river width and visibility are display controls.
- The earlier World Forge layout is restored: seed/resolution/season/generate header, left layer list, central globe, right inspector/parameters, and footer toggles. Narrow screens use side drawers. Reset view is retained; the N/S spin-axis indicator tracks the camera.
- Thirteen generation controls were added. Definitions and validation live together in `src/core/config.ts`. UI controls, inspection, worker lifecycle, particle animation and river ribbons are separate readable modules.

## Focused comparison

Seed `AETHER-2408`, Earth radius; default settings of each respective version. Arid land means PET / precipitation > 2.3. Confluences and longest connected paths use the **same 100 m³/s threshold** in both versions, so the difference is not merely the new, lower display threshold.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10,242 / 10,242 | 163 → 376 | 45.2% → 17.0% | 6 → 116 | 1,906 → 3,817 |
| 10,242 / 163,842 | 164 → 371 | 44.9% → 17.2% | 11 → 274 | 1,014 → 2,461 |
| 40,962 / 40,962 | 155 → 373 | 49.1% → 18.8% | 10 → 219 | 578 → 3,202 |

At the new 20 m³/s display threshold, the default world has 221 confluences and a longest connected path of about 4,132 km. Default runoff rises from about 9.9 to 87.7 mm/year. Surface refinement can resolve more small catchments; its graph is not expected to have an identical number of channels. The rainfall comparison isolates simulation refinement from surface-only refinement.

These are bounded observations for one seed at three relevant resolutions, not broad calibration or proof of scientific climate realism. Machine-readable observations are in `revision-2.1-measurements.json`. Time-step coefficient precomputation was added after these climate measurements without changing their equations; the final 40,962-cell process/surface run took 100.8 seconds on this machine (single observation, not a performance target). Its atmospheric mass residual was 2.13 × 10⁻¹¹.

## Verification and limits

Three small tests cover unequal-area water exchange, physical diffusion reach on two meshes, and one world’s geometry/water/reservoir contracts plus visible confluences. TypeScript and the production build are checked. No browser interaction or visual QA was performed.

The land–atmosphere feedback is deliberately one bounded pass. Lakes retain the existing lumped annual-equilibrium approximation; nested subcell sinks and exact distributary deltas remain outside this model. The 40,962-cell process grid can be slow because physical mixing requires smaller time steps. Keeping 10,242 process cells while increasing surface detail is the practical detailed-world setting.

Old v2.0 project JSON files are accepted, with defaults supplied for the added controls. They regenerate using v2.1 physics, so exact reproduction of a v2.0 world requires the v2.0 source.
