# v2.3: supported headwaters and riverbank vegetation

## Confirmed causes

The river renderer accepted every corner-to-receiver edge above an annual-discharge threshold. In AETHER-25711, lowering visibility to 1 m³/s exposed 327,585 thaw-capable land edges, including storm runoff across dry land. Small flows also shared a relatively large minimum rendered width.

The ecology model's old binary `riparian` flag only influenced one wetland classification rule. Continuous vegetation potential still depended entirely on local rainfall, temperature, and aridity. The natural surface did not read river access at all. A substantial imported river therefore did not support vegetation along its desert banks.

## Changes

- `land-water.ts` retains `channelRunoff`, the existing overflow and soil-drainage portion of total runoff. No new water is created, and total runoff, ET, erosion, reservoir balances, climate, and terrain are unchanged.
- `rivers.ts` selects channels on the existing `drainage.receiver` graph. A headwater needs 8,000 km² of upstream land and 1 m³/s of non-storm supply by default. Both are generation controls. Once a channel starts, it continues downstream and joins other tributaries; dry local climate does not erase water imported from upstream.
- Wet reservoir members are temporarily collapsed into a lake node for one topological accumulation pass. Actual lake outflow supplies the downstream catchment, and the visible spill remains the original receiver edge. Closed lakes terminate the network. This is linear work, with no recursive lake processing or alternate routing forest.
- `ecology.ts` estimates accessible riverbanks from channel supply, physical bank width, growing-season temperature, and bank height above water. The bank half-width is `clamp(200 + 60 * sqrt(supply), 200, 4000)` metres; access declines over a 40 m height scale. Cell vegetation is the area-weighted mixture of background and bank potential. Ocean, lake, and permanent-ice cells remain unvegetated. Regional biomes and aridity remain climate-based.
- Natural world and vegetation views draw soft green strips at these banks. They remain visible regardless of the river visibility filter or river-line toggle. Their display width is exaggerated alongside the water strips; the ecological coverage calculation uses physical metres. Small rivers now have a smaller minimum strip width, preserving the visual distinction from trunks.
- The cell inspector shows established channel discharge, upstream catchment area, vegetation potential, and bank coverage. Older project JSON files receive defaults for the new controls on load.

## Focused reproduction

Reproduced the supplied v2.2.1 AETHER-25711 definition at 655,362 surface cells and 40,962 simulation cells. The baseline took 106.7 s in this environment; most time remained in the two weather passes. Its complete snapshot was retained, then the new water bookkeeping, channel selection, and ecology were replayed on those same fields to avoid another expensive weather run. Recomputed total runoff matched the baseline exactly.

| Visibility | Previous eligible edges | Selected river edges | Change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| ≥1 m³/s | 327,585 | 62,015 | −81.1% |
| ≥20 m³/s | 126,278 | 51,923 | −58.9% |
| ≥100 m³/s | 36,172 | 26,806 | −25.9% |

Counts exclude ocean-only and permanently frozen segments; the diagnostic uses any thawing incident cell, whereas the renderer interpolates corner temperature. These are model eligibility measurements, not screenshot pixel counts.

At 1 m³/s, eligible edges touching desert cells fell from 91,006 to 6,771 (−92.6%). The selected network retains 1,192 confluences. All 6,032 desert cells with modeled banks gained vegetation potential: mean whole-cell cover rose from 2.69% to 6.81%, while mean bank potential was 90.2% over 4.48% of cell area. Cells away from modeled banks had no vegetation change. Channel selection took about 0.22 s; ecology about 0.16 s. These local timings are not browser performance guarantees.

Verification covers the existing geometry/water/lake contracts plus one focused fixture: supported sources merge, storm-only paths are omitted, channels cross a dry valley and spilling lake, imported water greens low desert banks, and high ridges/ocean/ice do not gain vegetation. Shader compilation and the production build cover the changed rendering integration; no browser visual review was performed.

## Scope

Four seasonal samples cannot establish daily or year-round stream permanence. The supply criterion is a simple worldbuilding channel-initiation model, not a calibrated river inventory. Ephemeral channels still exist in the full runoff graph and contribute water and erosion. Real drylands can contain many such channels; they should not all be portrayed as continuously flowing blue rivers. See [USGS on intermittent and ephemeral stream connectivity](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/hydrological-connectivity-intermittent-rivers-and-ephemeral-streams).

Bank vegetation is an access-based potential, not a coupled aquifer or irrigation simulation. It does not deduct additional evapotranspiration from river discharge. This localized representation is motivated by riparian dependence on river-connected alluvial groundwater; see [USGS on streamflow and riparian vegetation](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/fort-collins-science-center/science/streamflow-fluvial-geomorphology-riparian-vegetation). Channel source area, bank width, and access depth are explicit modeling choices rather than measured universal constants.
