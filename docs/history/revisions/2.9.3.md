# v2.9.3 — Continuous river ribbons without folded spans

The circled AETHER-0004 screenshot showed pinched and crossed river segments. The previous renderer rotated each sample's two sides independently and limited width from very short spans between curve samples. Those widths oscillated, and adjoining cross-sections could fold a quad into a bow tie. The retained world's water mesh had 25,952 such folded quads.

## Changes

- Walk visible reaches into connected main-channel ribbons. The strongest incoming tributary continues downstream; other tributaries meet its centerline with their own width and orientation. All ribbons remain batched in one mesh.
- Use rectangular/trapezoidal spans with one perpendicular at both ends. Small bevel triangles connect bends without extended corners or repeated width pinches. Straight continuations share their endpoint vertices.
- Simplify bends smaller than 35% of the displayed half-width, retaining every reach endpoint and confluence. This changes the display glyph, not the generated course or its measured sinuosity.
- Use direct anchored lake/ocean approaches so moving a mouth into visible water cannot turn it back through the old curve samples. Merge coincident samples instead of drawing zero-length spans.
- Preserve downstream animation phase at shared nodes, seasonal activity and source colors. Lakes, blended banks and seasonal delta-cell shading from v2.9.2 remain in place.

`RiverGeometry.ts` now handles course and bank lookup plus shoreline anchors. The obsolete frame/curvature-width machinery was deleted. `RiverRibbon.ts` contains simplification and the small span/bevel builder; `RiverStrips.ts` assembles visible river paths and their flow attributes. Generator identity remains 2.9.0.

## Focused verification

The retained AETHER-0004 snapshot was reused without re-running climate. At annual view, threshold 20 m³/s, width 1× and relief 10×, the revised globe water mesh has 3,159 main/tributary ribbons, 195,161 vertices and 152,336 triangles. It has zero reversed and zero degenerate triangles in the winding check. The prior water mesh had 710,764 vertices. New construction took 0.721 seconds in this environment.

All 23 focused tests pass. The revised join test verifies connected index topology; a tight-bend regression checks preserved water width and consistent nonzero triangle winding. Existing globe/map and physical invariants remain covered. TypeScript and the production build pass. These are code/geometry checks, not browser visual testing or GPU performance measurements.

## Scope

A branched river network is represented by connected main and tributary strokes; confluences overlap at the shared centerline rather than requiring an expensive polygon union. Bevels approximate corners, and sub-width bends are intentionally softened to the display scale. Physical flow, terrain, river systems, lakes and saved world definitions are unchanged.
