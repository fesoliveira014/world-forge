# v2.8.0 — visible river destinations and localized seasonal floodplains

AETHER-0003 exposed an inconsistency between water accounting, routing, and rendering. A broad, nearly level reservoir had a positive annual wet area, but its level equaled its sampled floor. Rivers terminated at modeled water while the depth-only renderer painted those points as land. The largest hidden entry carried **11,765.55 m³/s**. Channel infiltration and evaporation are not implemented, so losses were not a valid explanation.

## Changes

- `lake-surfaces.ts` gives balanced lake area a connected corner footprint, directs exposed equal-height ground toward that shore, and re-accumulates discharge once through real lake releases. `LakeSurfaces.ts` draws the footprint in both layouts even when bathymetry is unresolved. No lake depth or reservoir release is invented.
- Size labels distinguish Lake, Great lake (10,000 km²), and Inland sea (100,000 km²). These are naming conventions; salinity is not modeled. Inspectors show destination, area, inflow/outflow, basin-versus-water context, and unresolved-depth notices.
- The existing soil/snow loop retains four seasonal runoff rates. The river graph routes them, buffers releases using lake residence volume, and normalizes their mean to authoritative annual discharge. This estimates timing; it is not a seasonal lake-storage trajectory.
- `floodplains.ts` estimates each bank separately from seasonal overbank flow, a bounded fraction of reach transit volume, groundwater access distance, and valley slope. Steep terraces narrow the bank instead of suppressing vegetation immediately beside the channel.
- Regional biomes remain climate-based. Separate meadow, woodland, and wetland habitats fade into the surrounding terrain, including within deserts and existing forests. Physical fractions and vegetation potential are available for future settlement rules.
- A **Floodplains** layer shows selected-season overbank potential, or the seasonal peak in Annual view. Natural world, Biomes, and Vegetation show local banks even when river lines are hidden. River animation also responds to seasonal flow.

## Scope and checks

The full supplied configuration regenerated in 105.86 seconds (previous run: 106.37 seconds). All selected terminal paths reached an ocean mouth or wet lake footprint; the previously hidden 56 entries now have explicit water destinations. Ocean flow agreement was within 2.33e-10 m³/s and seasonal mean agreement within 5.77e-8 relative. Retained typed arrays increased from 442.9 MB to 487.6 MB. These are single-run observations, not performance targets.

The 17 existing core/projection checks passed with updated seasonal fixtures. Two added regressions cover area-preserving flat-floor routing and visible zero-depth water geometry on globe and map. TypeScript and the production build passed. No broad seed sweep or browser QA was added.

Floodplain parameters are uncalibrated worldbuilding estimates, not flood probability or a hydraulic forecast. Nutrient concentrations, atmospheric humidity feedback from rivers, channel water losses, daily floods, and irrigation are not simulated. Explicit shorelines do not resolve subcell lake bathymetry. See the [illustrated architecture](../../algorithms.md) for algorithms, formulas, diagrams, and limits.
