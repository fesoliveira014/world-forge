# v2.9.1 — River joins, lake surfaces, and delta island tiles

The AETHER-0004 screenshots showed projecting green bank ends, mismatched lake triangles, and deltas without recognizable land between channels. This rendering revision addresses those geometry paths. Generator identity remains 2.9.0; terrain, river receivers, discharge, lake storage, source classifications, and configuration are unchanged.

## Diagnosis and changes

| Visible problem | Code-level cause | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bank fingers and abrupt ribbon joins | Each reach chose its own endpoint normal and bank width. Only the water, not the full bank, was bounded at tight bends. | Share a node frame, interpolate width/color into the downstream section, constrain outer banks, and fade real corridor ends. |
| Lake seams, floating edges, and triangular fringes | Explicit lake patches used a second height construction; the natural material also interpolated an independent cell-depth water mask. Large layer lifts amplified globe separation. | Align patches with actual primal/dual surface faces, share Cartesian edges, draw inland water once, and use small ordered offsets. |
| Empty-looking delta fans | Flow branches existed without visible low banks between them; exaggerated river widths could occupy much of the fan. | Add up to four small vegetated bars per suitable fan, reserve distributary gaps, cap branch display widths to fan scale, and omit the obsolete terminal bank ribbon. |

The helper modules are `WaterSurface.ts` for local terrain draping and lake anchors, `RiverGeometry.ts` for common junction sections, and `DeltaTiles.ts` for the representative island mosaic. Existing geometry and palette modules consume these directly. There is no new simulation pass or render target.

## Focused verification

The exact supplied AETHER-0004 definition was regenerated once at 655,362 surface / 40,962 process cells. It contains 391 delta fans; the new presentation produces 1,431 bars/islands. All revised overlay coordinates are finite in both layouts. The two inspected lake cells retain their five-sixths coverage and unchanged physical fields.

All 23 tests passed, including a common-cross-section regression, shallow water visibility, lake vertices confined to their parent face, existing water budgets, and map seam handling. Globe construction took about 0.24 s for lakes, 2.69 s for banks, 0.82 s for river water, and 0.04 s for delta tiles in this environment. These are CPU geometry timings; browser visual testing and GPU frame-rate measurement were not performed.

## Scope

The lake shoreline remains limited by mesh resolution. Categorical and smooth layers use their own underlying triangulations; changing Globe/Flat map keeps the selected layer's footprint. Delta islands are representative subcell detail inside an existing delta fan, not extra physical terrain or a new water/land-area budget. Very narrow distributary gaps cannot fit an island. Settlement or navigation logic should continue using the generated physical fields.
