# v2.9.0 — River landscapes, wetlands, and deltas

River paths previously followed straight graph edges, incision was one small undated pass, and the snapshot did not retain water-source pathways or distinct local wetland classes. This revision adds those processes with direct modules and one final climate/drainage refresh.

## What changes

- **Terrain:** bounded downstream-first incision over a separate 0–5 Myr recent landscape interval. Excavated volume becomes local sediment, mouth deposits, or explicit unresolved export; land stays above the existing datum floor.
- **Courses:** valley space, slope, bank mobility, discharge, and interval length determine a bounded centerline sketch. Actual segment intersections produce cutoffs. The live channel and green banks share the resulting path and retain receiver endpoints.
- **Relict pools:** preserved horseshoes have a physical footprint and a rain/melt/ET/overflow bucket that replaces the corresponding soil area. They can be wet, frozen, or dry. Overflow uses existing routing.
- **Sources:** four disjoint delivery pathways follow the water budget: rapid rainfall, rapid snowmelt, slow soil release, and lake release. Mixtures merge at confluences; lake storage buffers the seasonal pattern. Wetland headwater is contextual. Flow regime comes from the four computed seasonal discharges.
- **Wetlands:** local marsh, swamp, fen, and bog potential depends on saturation, terrain, river/lake access, temperature, and vegetation. Deposited delta flats can receive bounded local river-access potential. Regional biomes and rainfall are unchanged by habitat classification.
- **Deltas:** transported sediment can build low mouth flats. Qualifying final mouths have two or three wet-connected branches whose discharge fractions sum to one. No duplicate water is added to the solver.

## Controls and views

Regenerate existing project definitions. Missing `fluvialDuration` and `bankMobility` receive defaults of 1 Myr and 1×. Erosion must be enabled for recent shaping; setting fluvial duration or mobility to zero disables the corresponding process. The three new layers are River sources, River landforms, and Marshes & peatlands, bringing the total to 27. All work in Globe and Flat map. Inspection shows source percentages, seasonal regime, sinuosity, cell-average lowering, mouth deposits, wetland coverage, cutoff depth, and branch shares when present.

## Scope

The current terrain resolution supports regional valleys and canyon-like incision, not subcell canyon walls. Meandering is a kinematic sketch constrained by valley conditions; age alone never assigns a river pattern. The assumed recent landscape interval is separate from plate evolution and is not a river birth date. Groundwater springs use slow soil release as a proxy; moving glaciers, aquifers, daily flow, peat chemistry, tidal deltas, and avulsion are not simulated. Cutoff pools currently receive rain and melt, not a separate river-flood recharge exchange. Wetland and delta vegetation remain habitat potential, not irrigation or another water withdrawal.

## Focused observations

All 22 tests passed, including the existing conservation and routing suite, the bounded-incision/sediment regression, geometric cutoffs, source fractions, delta shares, and both-layout overlay geometry. The complete AETHER-0003 reference generation at 10,242 / 2,562 cells took 1.63 s and contained 17 cutoff remnants and two deltas.

On a retained 655,362 / 40,962-cell AETHER-0003 world with climate held fixed, cutoff seeding took 0.083 s, incision/sediment 0.384 s, courses 0.240 s, and delta construction 0.055 s. That targeted run found 1,908 remnants (644 wet in at least one season), 428 delta fans, and 2.33% coverage-weighted local wetland area on non-ocean land. It retained about 505 MiB of typed arrays before render/GPU copies. This was a component check, not a complete new high-detail generation or a climate comparison. Counts and timings are observations for these inputs, not quotas or guarantees.
